Getting in

Sign in with your school account

Open the portal link and sign in with your @fis.edu Google account, the same one you use for school. That's the only time it asks who you are; everything after that just works.

  • Tap Sign in with Google and pick your school account.
  • If a page says it can't sign you in, you've probably opened it inside another app (Canvas, Gmail, Teams). Tap the ⋯ menu and choose Open in browser (Safari or Chrome).
🔒Your privacy: once you're in, your training is stored under an ID number, not your name or email, which keeps it private.

Session type

Training, practice or game?

Every session is one of three types. Pick it at the top of the session screen, and the screen changes to suit it.

Type

TrainingTeam practiceGame
  • Training: your own workout, with an exercise list you can swap and tick off.
  • Team practice: no exercise list; just log the time and how hard it was.
  • Game: a match or meet. Add the result if you want, and it never repeats weekly (it's a one-off).

Practices and games still count toward your training load. You'll spot them by their tag:

7

Your repeating week

Make it repeat

Your program is your repeating week. Mark a session “Part of my program” and it comes back automatically in future weeks.

  • Auto-repeat fills your empty future weeks from your program, so you're never staring at a blank week.
  • On a week that's only half full, “Fill this week from your program” tops up the missing days.
  • Change a repeating session (swap an exercise, change the plan) and it asks whether to save that to your program or keep it to this week.
💡Just logging your weights or reps never changes your program. That's your record of the session, not the plan. Only changes to the plan itself ask.
