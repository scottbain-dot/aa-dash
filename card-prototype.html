Prototype. The six qualities as a shape you own. Tap an attribute.

Why a shape. Its size is training age. Its shape is the coaching — spiky means imbalanced, and which spike tells you what to train. A quality never tested is a visible dent, which argues for turning up to testing far better than the words “not tested yet”. The faint outline is where you started, so getting better is the shape pushing outwards.
